# Third-Party Data Notices

This repository includes World Health Organization growth-reference data used
to calculate pediatric BMI-for-age z-scores.

- data/who_bmi_0_5.tsv is copied from the WHO Child Growth Standards data in
  the WorldHealthOrganization/anthro repository:
  data-raw/growthstandards/bmianthro.txt.
- data/who_bmi_5_19.tsv is copied from the WHO Growth Reference 2007 data in
  the WorldHealthOrganization/anthroplus repository:
  data-raw/growthstandards/bfawho2007.txt.

The files were retrieved on 2026-09-21 from the WHO-maintained GitHub
repositories. WHO is the source and rights holder for the reference material;
the repository's MIT license applies to this project's own code and does not
override rights or terms applicable to third-party WHO material.
